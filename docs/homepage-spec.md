# 🌿 SyncHer Homepage Specification v1.0

---

# Purpose

The homepage should introduce SyncHer as a premium women's wellness companion.

It should immediately communicate calmness, trust, simplicity, and emotional wellness.

Users should feel inspired to begin their wellness journey rather than feel like they are opening another health tracker.

---

# Overall Experience

The homepage should feel:

- Calm
- Premium
- Elegant
- Warm
- Nature-inspired
- Minimal
- Emotional
- Feminine through subtle design

The experience should focus on wellness, not statistics.

---

# Inspiration

Primary inspirations:

- MyFitnessPal (clean structure)
- Finch (warm emotional feeling)
- Calm (peaceful experience)
- Apple Health (clean layouts)
- Spark Wellness
- Premium wellness landing pages
- Nature-inspired editorial websites

# Visual Direction

Large amounts of white space.

Rounded cards.

Soft shadows.

Organic layouts.

# Images

Photography should include:

- Women journaling
- Herbal tea
- Reading
- Yoga
- Meditation
- Healthy meals
- Indoor plants
- Morning sunlight
- Cozy interiors

Avoid:

- Hospitals
- Gym equipment
- Neon colors
- Generic business stock photos

---

# UI Guidelines

Cards should feel elevated.

Buttons should have rounded corners.

Animations should be subtle.

Spacing should be generous.

Typography should remain readable.

Every section should breathe.

Avoid visual clutter.

---

# Success Criteria

A first-time visitor should immediately understand:

- What SyncHer is.
- Who it is designed for.
- How it improves daily wellness.
- Why it feels different from traditional health apps.

The homepage should feel calming, trustworthy, and premium.
