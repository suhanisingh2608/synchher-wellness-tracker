# 🌸 SyncHer

## Product Vision

SyncHer is a personalized women's wellness companion that helps users build healthier habits through mood tracking, hydration, sleep, movement, nourishing food guidance, and personalized wellness insights.

---

## Mission

Helping women understand their bodies and build healthier habits—one day at a time.

---

## Problem Statement

Many wellness apps only solve one problem.

Users switch between multiple apps for hydration, sleep, nutrition, fitness, and mindfulness.

SyncHer brings these experiences together in one calm, supportive platform.

---

## Target Users

- College students
- Working professionals
- Women aged 18–35
- Beginners in healthy living
